      The breach is easing but still stands, ~3pt over. Bad rate on the completed week Aug 10–16 is 17.9% on the rolling 28-day median, down from 20.0% — the first improvement after four consecutive worse readings since the Jul 5 baseline (~15.9 → ~17.9 → 20.0 → 20.0 → 17.9). The partial Aug 17–20 read looks even better (15.8% bad, 84.2% quality), but most of that is merchant mix, not quality: on a fixed set of 475 merchants present on both dates, the median is flat (84.4% → 85.0%), and the merchant count in the window dropped from ~545 to 495 as message-history lag holds back the last 1–2 days. Read the guardrail as stabilizing ~3pt over baseline, not fixed. The raw weekly aggregate mildly disagrees (9.4% → 9.9% bad on the partial period) — small moves, a watch item; the median tracks the typical merchant while the aggregate is dominated by the highest-volume merchants, and the guardrail is defined on the median. The reason mix improved in the right places: action-done-incorrectly fell hardest (11.1% → 4.7% of bad tickets, the biggest single improvement), knowledge reasons still dominate (ignored 38.5%, wrong 37.7%, missing 33.8% — merchants can pick several reasons, so shares sum past 100%). One flag: tone-of-voice complaints more than doubled (3.9% → 8.6%), on only 40 tickets — early signal, checking again next week. The Cortex probe into the breach (wrong-knowledge focus) is still open. Source: Cortex V3 merchant quality rate.
