      The breach stands at ~1.7pt over, flat for two weeks. Bad rate on the rolling 28-day median read 16.7% for both completed weeks Aug 16–22 and Aug 23–29, down from the 20.0% peak at the start of August and 17.9% on Aug 10–16 (20.0 → 20.0 → 17.9 → 16.7 → 16.7). Quality rate (good + ok) is 83.3% against 85.0% at baseline. The volume-weighted raw weekly bad rate is 9.3% (9.1% the week before), flat; the gap to the median is structural because the median weights every merchant equally and small merchants rate worse. Reason mix (now share of reason mentions, not comparable to the Aug 20 figures): wrong knowledge 18.8% (−1.8pt), ignored 15.8% (+0.6), missing 14.1% (+1.8, the fastest riser), hand over sooner 11.9%. A sample review of missing-knowledge tickets found stale content as the main theme (retired promotions, discontinued options, old stock states), plus policy gaps such as international duties and replacement parts; several merchants attached the exact text they want added. Suggested fix: prompt merchants to refresh time-sensitive guidance and surface staleness on resources cited in bad-rated tickets. The tone-of-voice spike flagged Aug 20 did not persist. Note on sources: the Aug 27 digest run read this same period at ~7.8% bad on roughly half the merchants; treated as a bad run, the Sep 3 run is back on the standard cut. The Cortex probe into the breach (wrong-knowledge focus) is still open. Source: Cortex V3 merchant quality rate.
